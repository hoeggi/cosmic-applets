Weather applet (Open-Meteo): design proposals

Three popup directions (A, B, C) plus the shared panel button, settings and states. Built with the shared mockup kit; invented data shaped like Open-Meteo responses. Add ?theme=light for the light theme.

Panel button

Popup: three proposals

A · Classic list is the familiar layout: conditions, six tiles, 7 days that expand into hours. B · Timeline is about what happens next: a rain alert, a 24-hour curve with a 3-hour table, optional air and pollen, and the same expandable days as A. C · At a glance plus air keeps the top short and adds air quality, UV and pollen.

  1. 1
    Header
    Name of the active location and the age of the last successful fetch. Refresh fetches now; the gear opens Settings (S1).
    saved location name · local fetch time
  2. 2
    Current conditions
    Weather icon, temperature now, a short description and the feels-like temperature. Today's sunrise and sunset on the right.
    current.temperature_2m, apparent_temperature, weather_code (icon + text), is_day (day/night icon) · daily.sunrise, daily.sunset
  3. 3
    Rain alert
    Only shown when rain is expected within the next 2 hours: the first 15-minute step with rain, rounded to the quarter hour. Hidden when it stays dry.
    first minutely_15.precipitation > 0 in the next 8 steps
  4. 4
    24-hour chart
    Line and shaded area: hourly temperature. Grey bars: hourly rain amount. Dots every 3 hours, dashed down to the table; the filled dot is now. Darker background is night. Hover shows any hour.
    hourly.temperature_2m, hourly.precipitation · sunrise/sunset for the night shading
  5. 5
    3-hour table
    One column per dot, directly beneath it: temperature, weather icon, rain chance (only from 20 %), and the time, which doubles as the chart's x-axis.
    hourly.temperature_2m, weather_code, precipitation_probability at every third hour
  6. 6
    Air (optional)
    Off by default; switched on under Settings → Popup → Air quality and UV. European AQI on its banded scale, PM2.5 and NO₂, and today's highest UV. Turning it on adds a second request per refresh.
    air-quality-api: current european_aqi, pm2_5, nitrogen_dioxide · daily uv_index_max from the forecast
  7. 7
    Pollen (optional)
    Off by default; Settings → Popup → Pollen. Four common Austrian pollen types, bucketed from grains/m³ into None / Low / Moderate / High. Europe only, so the toggle is hidden elsewhere.
    air-quality-api: current alder_pollen, birch_pollen, grass_pollen, ragweed_pollen
  8. 8
    Next 7 days
    Same as A: day, icon, rain amount, low, range bar and high on one shared scale. Tap a day to open its hours in 3-hour steps; one day is open at a time. Today starts closed because the chart already covers it. Shown here with Wed open.
    daily.weather_code, precipitation_sum, temperature_2m_min / _max · hourly values for the opened day
  9. 9
    Attribution
    Required by the CC BY 4.0 licence of Open-Meteo's data.
